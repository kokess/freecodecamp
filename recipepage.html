Classic Pancake Recipe

This classic pancake recipe is quick, easy, and perfect for a delicious breakfast or brunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Add the milk and egg, then whisk until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour batter onto the pan and cook until bubbles form.
  5. Flip and cook until golden brown.
